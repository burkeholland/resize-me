# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows and macOS users who frequently resize the foreground application for focused work, demos, testing, or repeatable desktop layouts. They need to choose a trusted size, trigger it immediately or with a global shortcut, and adjust app behavior without learning a complex window-management tool.

## Product Purpose

ResizeMe makes repeatable window sizing fast and dependable. Success means users can understand the active resize behavior at a glance, complete common setup tasks in the section where they expect them, and trust that their presets and preferences will persist locally.

## Brand Personality

Native, focused, and reassuring. Each app should feel purpose-built for its host platform: familiar enough to use without instruction, precise enough to trust, and calm enough to stay out of the user's way.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dense control wall with weak hierarchy. Do not make every option compete equally for attention, bury frequent tasks among unrelated controls, or import Windows conventions into the native macOS app (or macOS conventions into the Windows app).

## Design Principles

- Organize around user intent, not implementation details.
- Keep the active resize action and current selection immediately understandable.
- Use familiar platform-native interaction patterns and explicit labels.
- Reveal complexity where it is needed instead of presenting every control at once.
- Preserve user trust through predictable feedback and backward-compatible settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support complete keyboard operation with a logical document and focus order, visible focus treatment, explicit accessible names, and no interaction that depends on hover alone. Maintain WCAG 2.1 AA text contrast, honor reduced-motion preferences, and ensure state is never conveyed by color alone.
